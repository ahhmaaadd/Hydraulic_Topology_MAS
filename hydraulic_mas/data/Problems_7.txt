1. Design the hydraulic system for a horizontal machine-tool slide. At the start of each cycle, the slide must travel 250 mm toward the workpiece against a 15 kN resistance in 5 seconds. It must then continue through a 100 mm cutting stroke against a 35 kN resistance at an adjustable feed speed between 0.5 and 1.0 m/min. The change from approach to cutting feed must occur automatically at the 250 mm position without stopping the slide. After machining, the slide must retract the complete 350 mm against a 5 kN resistance in no more than 5 seconds. Cutting-load variation of plus or minus 15 percent must not materially change the selected feed speed. Maximum system pressure is 70 bar.

2. Design the hydraulic system for a vertical production press with a total downward travel of 1.0 m. The ram must travel the first 0.80 m at 5 m/min against negligible external resistance, the next 0.12 m at 2 m/min against 12 kN, and the final 0.08 m at 2 m/min while developing 50 kN. It must then return the full 1.0 m in no more than 8 seconds against a 3 kN resistance and remain securely at the upper position for at least 10 minutes with the power unit unloaded. The moving weight is already included in the stated loads. Maximum system pressure is 100 bar.

3. Design the hydraulic system for a horizontal positioning fixture. The moving carriage must travel 300 mm, advance against 15 kN at 3.5 m/min, and return against 9.5 kN at 5.5 m/min. The operator must be able to stop at any point and hold the carriage for 10 minutes under the full stated load with negligible drift. Releasing the command must place the system in a safe neutral condition. Maximum system pressure is 100 bar.

4. Design the hydraulic system for a machine slide that travels 200 mm toward a workpiece against 2.5 kN at approximately 1.5 m/min. Once it contacts the workpiece, the resisting load rises to 12.5 kN and the slide must continue through a 100 mm working movement at approximately 0.5 m/min. It then returns through the complete 300 mm travel against 2.5 kN. The speed change must result from the change in load without using an electrical pressure switch. Maximum system pressure is 20 bar.

5. Design the hydraulic system for a CNC drilling machine. The workpiece must first be clamped through a 20 mm movement at 1.5 m/min while developing approximately 4000 N. Only after the clamp has reached the workpiece and developed the required force may the drilling motion begin. The drilling motion must travel 100 mm at 0.1 m/min against 2500 N. During return, the drill must retract before the clamp releases. Maximum system pressure is approximately 8 bar, and the drilling transition occurs after clamping pressure reaches approximately 3.3 bar. No electrical sensing device is to be used.

6. Design the hydraulic system for a wide rigid platen driven at two points. The two sides must remain synchronized because they are joined by the same rigid structure. The platen must move 500 mm against a total load of 40 kN in 5 seconds. It must return through 500 mm against a total load of 12 kN as quickly as the same pump permits. Maximum pressure is 80 bar.

7. Design a hydraulic system for a machine station with two linear motions. The first motion must travel 50 mm and clamp the part with approximately 12 kN. Clamping pressure must not exceed 40 bar. Once full clamping force is established, a second motion must automatically advance 200 mm in approximately 4 seconds and develop 30 kN at up to 60 bar. During return, the working motion must retract before the clamp is released. Overall pressure must not exceed 70 bar, and no electrical pressure switch may be used.
